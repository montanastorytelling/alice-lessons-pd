**Time: 2 hours min**

## Teachers Experience

Read through class period two (3.3 on lesson plan) and answer the following questions:
- How will you implement this in your classroom?
- What challenges might you face?
- How will you ensure every student has a voice?

Journal down answers to these questions, as they may help during a class session.

## Students Experience

Pull up the Alice starter world, and begin animating a story. As you go through this, complete [worksheet 3](https://montanastorytelling.github.io/alice-lessons/beaded-bags/worksheet_3),
 as your students will be completing these tasks in tandem. Ask yourself the following questions:
 - What questions might students have during the activity?
 - What questions might students have on the worksheet 3?
 - How do you resolve a conflict, if students can't decide what to animate?
 - How do you deal with a student that doesn't want to pair up?
 
 Journal down your answers to these questions, as they may help during class time.
