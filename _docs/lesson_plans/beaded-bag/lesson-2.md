**Time: 50 min**

On worksheet 2, you came up with a plan for how your animation would work. 
Spend some time attempting to animate that. If you are in a group you may 
need to discuss which parts from each persons steps will be added. See if 
you can follow along with the animation in real time, seeing how the elements 
reflect your code. During this period, make sure to fill out [worksheet 3](https://montanastorytelling.github.io/alice-lessons/beaded-bags/worksheet_3).

> If you are in a classroom environment or in a group, once you feel your animation is complete,
 share your animation with others when appropriate and describe your story. Invite others to describe 
differences between your story and theirs.

Complete [worksheet 4](https://montanastorytelling.github.io/alice-lessons/beaded-bags/worksheet_4) on your own time.
