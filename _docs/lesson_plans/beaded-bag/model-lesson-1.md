**Time: 50 Min**

First, summarize the reading. Then answer the following questions:

- How long do you think it takes to make a piece of beadwork? How long do you think it took someone to make a beaded bag?
- What tribes are known for their beadwork in Montana
- Where is the Columbia Plateau?
- How has beadwork changed through time?
- How did the economy influence changes in beadwork?
- What modern beadwork have you seen? What does it look like? Who made it? Who uses it or where is it
displayed? Was it made for art or for function?

The following questions should also be considered on the beaded bagon page 8 of the reading:

- When was the bag most likely created?
- What can you tell me about the landscape?
- What do you think the person on the horse is holding?
- Who do you believe this man is? Why? What is he wearing?
- Who do you believe the woman on the right is? What is she wearing?

With the following questions, you may pair up with a partner or single handedly journal.

- What story do you think is unfolding in the scene depicted on the bag?
- What part of the story is being depicted?
Why is the man on a horse? Is he leaving or arriving?
- Are the two people in the scene strangers or acquaintances?
- What is the woman holding?
- Did she give or recieve the gift?

> If in a group, you can take time to share your ideas with others and discuss.
