**Time: 1 hour**

## Teacher's Experience

Read through Class Period Two (3.3 in lesson plan) and consider the following questions:
- How will you implement this in your classroom?
- What challenges might you face?
- How will you ensure every student has a voice?

Journal down answers to these questions, as they may help during a class session.

## Student's Experience

Open up the Horses starter world and walk through the discussion questions the students are 
required. Follow along with the lesson and ask yourself these questions:
- What questions are easier/harder to answer from the student's perspective?
- How can you facilitate questions that students may have?
- What parts of Alice may be hard for students to understand when interacting with it?
- How can you make up this difference as a teacher?

Journal down answers to these questions, as they may help during a class session.
